# Generative art workflows

Create a self-contained visual that runs locally in a browser. Begin with the requested mood, subject, format, and output size; select Canvas, SVG, or p5.js according to the effect rather than a fixed template.

## Build

- Define a small palette, composition rules, and a deterministic seed before drawing. Expose the seed and the few parameters that materially alter the result.
- Use layers: background, structural forms, texture, and focal details. Give each layer a distinct visual purpose.
- Keep controls legible and separate from the artwork. Include reset, regenerate, and export only when they help the requested experience.
- Write original algorithms and shapes. Do not reproduce an artist's distinctive work, a logo, or a third-party brand system.

## Verify

1. Open the result in a browser at the target viewport.
2. Regenerate several times and confirm that the seed recreates an identical image.
3. Check contrast, clipping, performance, and export dimensions.
4. Deliver the runnable source and any exported asset requested by the user.

## Handoff

State the visual concept, technology used, seed behavior, and the adjustable parameters. Keep external dependencies explicit and minimal.
